# Project Context — who CBC is

**CBC** = Construction Building Components, the national-accounts division of
**The Hamilton Parker Company**, 1865 Leonard Ave, Columbus OH.

CBC quotes and supplies commercial building components to **general contractors,
franchisees, and architects**, with a strong focus on retail and quick-serve /
fast-casual restaurant chains. It is backed by in-house estimating, a warehouse, and
door fabrication.

## Boundaries
- Estimating modelled here lives **inside CBC national accounts only**. Other HP divisions
  (showroom, tile, masonry, fireplaces, garage doors) run separately and are out of scope.
  The approach can extend to them later.
- CBC sells to the **GC / internal initiator**, not to the architect and not to the end customer.
- Quotes are **supply-only material** — no installation labor, no turnkey scopes.
  See [[sales_tax_rules]].

## Who requests a bid
Requests come from the **internal initiator in the sales queue** — Kellan, Matt, Rebecca,
or Tina — shown far-right in the queue view. The finished quote goes back to *that specific
person*, never to a group email. See [[process_flow]] Phase 6.

## Intake channels
Mostly **email** (job workbook + plans/RFP attached). Sometimes **phone-in**, which is why
a "create new bid request" path exists (NR-5).

## Knowledge-continuity risk
Estimating knowledge is concentrated in three people — Kevin, Rick, Shanna
(see [[estimator_profiles]]). Capturing their rules and reference data into this system is
an explicit goal, not a side effect. This is a long-term engagement with ongoing
maintenance, not a one-and-done build.
