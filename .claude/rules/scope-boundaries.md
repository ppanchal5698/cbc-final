# Scope Boundaries

Quote what is in scope. **Do not attempt to price anything on the out-of-scope list** — flag
it as out of scope and move on.

## IN SCOPE
- **Doors**: metal and wood; HP-Fabrication doors
- **Frames**: hollow metal, welded/loaded and knock-down
- **Door hardware**: hardware sets, grade 1 and 2, quoted by part number / series
- **Division 10 specialties**
- **Restroom / toilet partitions**
- **Restroom accessories**
- **Washroom equipment / hand dryers**
- **FRP wall panels**

## OUT OF SCOPE — do not quote
| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| Ceiling tile and grid | Another HP department / showroom |
| Tile | Another HP department / showroom |
| Thin brick, masonry | Another HP department / showroom |
| Aluminum / glass storefront | Not a CBC estimating category |
| Coiling / overhead / oversized doors | Separate HP division (garage doors) |
| Engineered wood | Confirmed out, 14 Jul |
| Metal siding / extruded aluminum | Confirmed out, 14 Jul |
| JL Industries access doors and specialties | Not CBC estimating |
| **Scranton Products** | **Access lost** — would have to go through a costlier distributor |
| **American Dryer** | **No longer used** — use World Dryer or Excel XLERATOR |

## Handling an out-of-scope item found in a bid set
1. Record it in extracted/scope_summary.json under out_of_scope_items with its source_page.
2. Do **not** create a line item, do **not** price it.
3. Note it in the review summary so the estimator can tell the GC what CBC is not covering.

The storefront in the Dutch Bros fixture (Kawneer 541T) is a worked example: it appears in the
drawings, it is read, and it is deliberately **not** quoted.
