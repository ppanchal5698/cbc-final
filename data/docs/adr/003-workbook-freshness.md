# ADR-003: Workbook governs P21 freshness

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Matrix 6.2 defaults: unreliable past ~6–8 months (code uses 6), discard past
3–4 years (code uses 36 months). Configurable via Settings.

## Consequences

Replaces the old 24/30-month defaults. Catalog price-sheet staleness stays at
24 months (Matrix 6.3 / stewardship), separate from PO freshness.
